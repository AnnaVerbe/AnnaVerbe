I am Anna Verbe, 🔭 I'm currently working on Insect's mechanosensors. I previously worked on developing 2-photon imaging with in vivo optogenetic stimulation of the larval brain of Drosophila melanogaster, on how locomotor output is structured by precise sensory input and on the Aerial righting reflex of hoverflies. My work includes mechanosensors, neuroethology, locomotion and neurosciences.
